Somewhere

Procedurally generated watercolor paintings of places that feel familiar.

Every painting is new. A building is designed from the architectural rules of a real place: its plot widths and storey heights, its materials and openings, its roofs, signs, plants and street life. Then it is set out in pencil, drawn in pen and painted in watercolour, live in your browser. Nothing is stored, and no two visitors see the same building.
